Peak Travel Calendar — Kent

When Transfers Book Up Fast from Langton Green

Understanding the peaks helps you book ahead and avoid being stranded. These are the busiest periods for airport taxis from Tunbridge Wells and the surrounding area.

☀️

July–August (School Summer)

The busiest airport period. Gatwick handles peak family departures. Early morning pickups (3am–6am) to Gatwick and Heathrow fill up weeks in advance. Book as early as possible.

🎃

October Half Term

Family and short-break travellers head for Gatwick and Stansted. Easyjet and Ryanair peak dates coincide with M25 roadworks more often than not. Allow extra buffer time.

🎄

Christmas & New Year

Heathrow is the busiest long-haul airport in the UK at Christmas. Domestic return traffic creates congestion on the A21 and M25 between 22–31 December. Plan pickup 30 minutes earlier than usual.

🌸

Easter & May Bank Holidays

Cruise departures from Dover spike dramatically around Easter — P&O and MSC both operate full sailings. Southampton transfers also peak. Book cruise port taxis at least 3 weeks in advance.

Route intelligence included with every booking: When you book with us, we factor in school pickup windows along Pembury Road, traffic on the A26 through Tonbridge, and M25 contraflow schedules when planning your departure time. This is the difference between making your flight and missing it by 20 minutes.

Airport Taxi FAQs — Langton Green & Tunbridge Wells

How much is a taxi from Langton Green to Gatwick Airport?+

A taxi from Langton Green to Gatwick is approximately £75–£85 for a standard saloon. The journey is roughly 32 miles via the A264 and M23. Your exact price is confirmed when you book — from a fixed starting price of £75, door to terminal. Gatwick has both North and South Terminals; we confirm which one your airline uses and drop you at the correct entrance.

How much is a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Heathrow Airport?+

A taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Heathrow Airport is typically £120–£135 for a saloon. The route goes via the A21 to the M25 (junction 5) and then west on the M25 to the M4 slip for Heathrow. Travel time is usually 75–100 minutes outside peak hours — we recommend allowing 2 hours if your flight departs between 6am and 9am due to M25 traffic around the Godstone and Clacket Lane sections.

Do you track flights for delays?+

Yes, always. We monitor your flight number in real time for both departures and arrivals. If your inbound flight is delayed, your driver adjusts automatically — you're never charged for waiting caused by airline delays. We ask for your flight number at the time of booking, and we monitor it from the moment you provide it.

Can you pick me up at 3am or 4am?+

Yes. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Early morning pickups — 3am, 4am, 5am — are among our most common bookings from the Langton Green and Tunbridge Wells area. The road is clear at that hour and the journey to Gatwick typically takes 35–40 minutes at that time of day. We simply ask that you book in advance so we can confirm your driver and vehicle.

Do you provide child seats?+

Yes, free of charge. We carry infant carriers, child seats and booster seats. When you book, tell us the ages of the children travelling and we'll fit the correct seat. Child seats are fitted before your driver arrives at your address — not installed in the car park at the airport.

What areas do you cover for pickups?+

We pick up from Langton Green, Tunbridge Wells, Tonbridge, Sevenoaks, Edenbridge, Penshurst, Speldhurst, Groombridge, Rusthall, Crowborough, Uckfield, East Grinstead, Forest Row and surrounding villages in Kent, Surrey and East Sussex. If you're unsure whether we cover your address, call or WhatsApp us — we'll confirm within minutes.

How far in advance should I book?+

For regular transfers, 24–48 hours notice is usually sufficient. For peak periods — school summer holidays (July–August), Christmas and New Year, Easter, and May bank holidays — we recommend booking 1–3 weeks ahead. Early morning slots (3am–6am) to Gatwick and Heathrow fill up particularly fast during school holidays.

Is the price the same regardless of time of day?+

Yes. Our fixed prices apply 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. There are no peak surcharges, no night rates and no Sunday premiums. The price you're quoted when you book is the price you pay when you arrive.
