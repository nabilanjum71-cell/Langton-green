How Your Airport Transfer Works — Start to Finish

Six steps. No surprises. This is how every booking works — whether it is a 4am Gatwick departure or a Southampton cruise return.

The Six Steps of Every Transfer

1

You Contact Us — Phone or WhatsApp (2 minutes)

Call 07808 065494 or send a WhatsApp message with your pickup address, destination airport or port, travel date, approximate time and flight or ship number. That is all we need to give you an immediate quote. No online forms, no 24-hour waiting period, no call centres. You speak directly to the person managing your booking.

2

Your Fixed Price Is Confirmed Immediately

We calculate your fare as a fixed price based on your exact pickup address and terminal entrance. This price is confirmed verbally and then in writing — by WhatsApp or email — so you have it in black and white before you commit to anything. The price you receive at this moment is the price you pay on the day. No meter running, no peak-time adjustment, no traffic surcharge. If the M25 adds 40 minutes to your Heathrow journey, you still pay the confirmed price.

3

We Calculate Your Pickup Time

This is where local knowledge matters. We look at your flight or ship departure time, add the check-in window your airline or cruise line requires, factor in the journey time from Langton Green or your specific address, and then add a sensible buffer for the time of day and season. We tell you what time to be ready — you do not have to calculate this yourself or worry whether you have allowed enough time. If you prefer to leave earlier for peace of mind, we accommodate that too.

For Gatwick morning departures, we typically plan for 15 minutes earlier than the minimum needed. For Heathrow during rush hour, we plan for 30 minutes of buffer. For cruise port transfers where check-in windows are specific, we work back from the latest acceptable terminal arrival.

4

Your Driver Arrives at Your Address — On Time

Your driver arrives at your home or pickup address at the agreed time. The vehicle is clean, the boot is ready, and your driver loads your luggage. You do not carry bags to a road end or wait at a communal pickup point. We come to your door — whether that is a house on the Langton Green village green, a cottage off a lane in Speldhurst, or a hotel in central Tunbridge Wells.

We do not do late pickups. If there is any reason your driver might be delayed — which is extremely rare — we call you in advance. Your departure time is not a variable we leave to chance.

5

Terminal Drop-Off at the Correct Entrance

We drop you at the correct terminal entrance for your airline or cruise line. Not the general airport perimeter — the specific drop-off zone for your departure building. For Heathrow, this means the correct terminal access road (T5 via J14 spur, not the main tunnel). For Gatwick, we confirm North or South Terminal before we leave Langton Green. For Dover, we head to the Eastern Docks cruise terminal, not the Western ferry entrance.

Your driver unloads your luggage at the kerbside. Payment is made at this point at the confirmed price. No card machine issues, no arguing about a changed fare — the price was confirmed at booking and it has not changed.

6

Return: Flight Tracked, Driver Meets You in Arrivals

For return transfers, we monitor your flight number from the moment it departs its origin airport. If your flight from Malaga is delayed by 90 minutes, we know before you do — and your driver adjusts accordingly. No extra charge, no frantic rearranging on your end.

Your driver will be in the arrivals hall of the correct terminal with a board bearing your name. For cruise returns from Dover or Southampton, we track the ship's port arrival and work with you on your disembarkation group timing. You walk out of customs and your driver is the first face you see. That is exactly how it should work.

What happens if my plans change? Contact us as soon as possible. For flight number changes, we update the tracking information immediately. For date or time changes, we accommodate these where availability allows. For cancellations, we ask for 24 hours notice for a full refund where a deposit has been paid.

What Makes This Process Different

Most taxi companies tell you they are reliable. The proof is in the detail of how they handle the things that go wrong — delayed flights, changed terminal assignments, early morning pickups on dark winter mornings. Our process is built around those moments specifically.

  • Fixed price confirmed in writing before any commitment — no verbal promises only
  • Pickup time calculated for you — not a figure you have to guess and hope is right
  • Driver arrives at your door — not at the end of your road or in a nearby car park
  • Terminal drop-off at the correct entrance — not just the airport boundary
  • Flight and ship tracking is active from departure, not from landing
  • Delay waiting at no extra charge — airline delays are not your financial problem
  • Name board in arrivals — a named pickup, not a phone call from a car park
