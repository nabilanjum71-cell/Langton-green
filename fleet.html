Our Fleet — Right Vehicle for Every Journey

Clean, comfortable, licenced private hire vehicles for airport transfers and cruise port runs. Tell us your group size and luggage and we'll match you to the right vehicle at the same fixed rate.

What Every Vehicle Includes

Standard across our entire fleet — no extras to request, no upgrade charges for the basics.

❄️

Air Conditioning

All vehicles have functioning air conditioning. For early morning summer departures to Gatwick, this matters more than it sounds — a hot car at 4am with tired children is nobody's idea of a good start.

🔌

USB Charging Points

USB-A and USB-C charging available in all vehicles. Business travellers can charge laptops and phones en route. Families can keep devices alive for the flight.

🧳

Luggage Loaded by Driver

Your driver loads and unloads all luggage at both ends of the journey. No self-loading, no extra charge for heavy bags. We handle everything at your front door and at the terminal kerb.

👶

Child Seats — Free

Infant carriers, rear-facing child seats, forward-facing seats and booster seats available at no charge. Fitted before the driver arrives at your address. Tell us ages when booking.

🧼

Cleaned Before Every Journey

Vehicles are cleaned between every transfer. No leftover cups, no crumbs from a previous family, no cigarette smell. You start your journey in a clean vehicle — the way it should be.

🗺️

Live Navigation

Real-time traffic monitoring with live route adjustment. If the M25 is stationary at Junction 7, we know before we get there and adjust. Your driver is never the last to know about an incident ahead.

Choosing the right vehicle: If you are unsure which vehicle to book, tell us your passenger count and describe your luggage — number of suitcases and approximate size. We recommend the right vehicle for your load. There is no benefit to us in sending a larger vehicle than you need; we give honest advice and confirm the correct option at the same fixed-price structure.

Specialist Equipment and Unusual Luggage

Not all airport passengers travel with standard suitcases. We regularly handle:

  • Golf bags: Estate and MPV vehicles accommodate full sets with bag carriers
  • Ski equipment: Roof bars available on request for ski/snowboard bags and boot bags
  • Pushchairs and prams: Folding and full-size prams accommodated — confirm model when booking
  • Wheelchairs: Folding wheelchairs fit in estate and MPV boots — mobility scooters require advance notice
  • Musical instruments: Cases and flight cases accommodated by arrangement
  • Oversized or heavy items: Call us to discuss — we find a solution or tell you honestly if we cannot
