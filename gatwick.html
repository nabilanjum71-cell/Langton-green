Gatwick Airport Taxi from Langton Green & Tunbridge Wells

Fixed-price transfer to Gatwick North and South Terminal. ~32 miles via A264 and M23. Flight tracked, meet & greet included. Call us for your confirmed price.

The Most Popular Airport Run from Langton Green

Gatwick is by far the most frequently requested airport transfer we make from Langton Green and the wider Tunbridge Wells area. It is close enough that a pre-dawn departure does not feel punishing, yet far enough that the route, the traffic and the terminal layout genuinely matter for getting there on time.

The route takes you along the A264 heading west through Langton Green village, past the Hare pub on the corner, then joins the A264 towards East Grinstead before connecting with the M23. From Junction 10 on the M23 the airport is signposted and takes around ten minutes in clear conditions. Gatwick is straightforward to reach from our area — but only if you know which terminal you are headed for before you hit the airport road system.

North Terminal vs South Terminal — Why It Matters

Gatwick's two terminals are separated by the railway station and a short transit journey. If your driver drops you at the wrong terminal, you face the monorail link, a queue and potential stress when you should be checking in. We always confirm which terminal your airline operates from before we leave Langton Green.

✈️ North Terminal

easyJet, British Airways (select routes), TUI, Jet2, Norwegian. Drop-off is directly at the terminal forecourt on the upper level.

✈️ South Terminal

Wizz Air, Vueling, BA (most routes), Virgin Atlantic, Delta. Drop-off is on the covered upper deck, directly into departures.

When you book, give us your flight number and we will confirm the terminal automatically — so you never have to look it up yourself. For return collections, we meet you in the arrivals hall of the correct terminal, name board in hand, regardless of which one your flight lands at.

Getting Your Timing Right for Gatwick Departures

The A264 from Langton Green is generally a smooth dual carriageway for most of its length, but the junction at Pembury Road and the approach towards East Grinstead can slow in rush hour. The M23 itself is rarely congested outside the 7am–9am and 4pm–6pm windows, but when a slip road closes at Junction 9 or 10 (which happens for overnight maintenance more often than you might expect), the diversion adds meaningful time.

Our standard guidance for Gatwick departures from Langton Green:

  • Pre-6am departures: Allow 45 minutes door to terminal. Roads clear, journey smooth.
  • 6am–9am departures: Allow 70–80 minutes. This is peak M23 time and the terminal forecourt is busiest.
  • 9am–3pm departures: Allow 55–65 minutes. Roads typically clear after the morning peak.
  • 3pm–7pm departures: Allow 75 minutes. Afternoon school run and southbound M23 traffic.
  • Evening departures (after 7pm): Allow 50–55 minutes. Roads usually clear again.

We calculate your recommended pickup time when you book — you do not have to work this out yourself. Just give us your departure time and we handle the timing.

Gatwick Return Transfers — Flight Tracked, Driver Waiting

Arriving at Gatwick after a long flight and emerging into arrivals to find nobody there is one of those genuinely demoralising experiences. It should never happen with a professional transfer service. We monitor your inbound flight from its point of departure — whether that is Malaga, Dubai or New York — and your driver arrives at the terminal before your flight lands, account for baggage reclaim time.

For arrivals, your driver will be in the arrivals hall with a board bearing your name. You do not need to find a car park, wait for a text, or navigate to a meeting point zone. Walk out of customs, see your name, and you are on your way home to Langton Green or Tunbridge Wells.

If your flight is delayed by two hours, three hours, or more — your driver is informed automatically and adjusts. You are never charged extra for airline-caused delays.

Why Not Drive and Park at Gatwick?

Many travellers from Langton Green and Tunbridge Wells consider driving themselves to Gatwick and using the long-stay or meet-and-greet car parks. It is worth understanding the real cost before deciding.

Gatwick long-stay parking currently costs in the region of £70–£120 for a week, depending on advance booking and the car park selected. Premium meet-and-greet parking runs £130–£180 for the same period. Add in fuel — roughly £10–£15 each way from Langton Green — and the cost of returning to find your car after a red-eye flight, the comparison shifts significantly. A fixed-price return transfer with door-to-door service is frequently comparable in cost and is vastly more convenient, particularly for families who would otherwise be loading a car at 4am.

Gatwick-specific tip from our drivers: Gatwick South Terminal long-term car park operates a shuttle bus that runs until the early hours but stops temporarily between roughly 3:30am–4:30am. Passengers who book a 5am departure sometimes arrive at a shuttle stop with no bus. Our transfer drops you directly at the terminal doors at any hour — there is no shuttle involved.

Gatwick Airport Taxi FAQs

How long does it take to drive from Langton Green to Gatwick?+

The drive from Langton Green to Gatwick takes approximately 40–55 minutes in normal conditions via the A264 and M23. Allow 60–75 minutes for departures between 6am and 9am when the M23 near Crawley experiences peak traffic. We calculate your recommended pickup time when you book.

How much is a taxi from Langton Green to Gatwick Airport?+

A taxi from Langton Green to Gatwick Airport costs approximately £75–£85 for a standard saloon at our fixed from-£75 rate. Your exact price is confirmed when you book based on your precise pickup address. There are no hidden charges, no peak surcharges and no meter running.

Do you cover Gatwick North Terminal and South Terminal?+

Yes. We drop off and collect at both terminals. At the time of booking, give us your flight number and we confirm which terminal your airline uses. We drop you at the correct level and zone for departures, and for returns we meet you in the right arrivals hall.

What if my return flight from Gatwick is delayed?+

We monitor your inbound flight in real time. If your flight is delayed by any amount, your driver adjusts automatically. You are never charged waiting time for airline-caused delays. Our driver will be in the arrivals hall when you come through customs, regardless of when your flight actually lands.

Can I book a Gatwick transfer for early morning, like 3am or 4am?+

Yes. Early morning pickups are our most common Gatwick bookings. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. A 3am pickup from Langton Green means a 3:40am–3:45am arrival at Gatwick, well ahead of most 6am departures. We simply ask you to book in advance so we can confirm your driver and vehicle.
