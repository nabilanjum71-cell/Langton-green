Airport Taxi FAQs

Common questions about booking airport transfers from Langton Green, Tunbridge Wells and Kent. Not answered here? Call or WhatsApp and we'll answer in minutes.

Pricing

Pricing & Payments

How much is a taxi from Langton Green to Gatwick Airport?+

Approximately £75–£85 for a standard saloon at our fixed £2.50/km rate. The journey is about 32 miles via the A264 and M23. Your exact price is calculated from your door to the terminal entrance and confirmed when you book — not when you land.

How much is a taxi from Tunbridge Wells to Heathrow Airport?+

Approximately £120–£135 for a standard saloon. The route goes via the A21, M25 Junction 5 and M4 to Heathrow. Your confirmed price includes the correct terminal drop-off — no extra charge for T5 via the Junction 14 spur.

Is the price really fixed? What if there are traffic delays?+

Yes. Genuinely fixed. The price is £2.50/km from your door to your terminal, calculated at booking and confirmed in writing. If the M25 adds 45 minutes to your Heathrow journey, you pay the same confirmed price. There are no peak surcharges, no night rates, no traffic supplements and no waiting charges for airline delays. The number in your confirmation is the number you pay.

How do I pay?+

Payment is made at the end of the journey in cash or by bank transfer. We can provide an invoice and VAT receipt for business travellers who need to claim on expenses. We do not accept card payments at the vehicle currently — please have cash or be prepared to transfer on the day.

Is there a booking fee or deposit?+

For most standard bookings, no deposit is required. For longer transfers (Stansted, Luton, Southampton) or peak period bookings where driver commitment is required well in advance, we may ask for a small deposit to secure the booking. This is confirmed at the time of booking.

Availability

Hours & Availability

Can you do early morning pickups at 3am or 4am?+

Yes. We operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Early morning pickups — 3am, 4am and 5am — are among our most frequently booked slots from Langton Green and Tunbridge Wells, particularly for Gatwick and Heathrow. A 4am pickup from Langton Green puts you at Gatwick by 4:40–4:50am in clear conditions. We simply ask you to book in advance so we can confirm your driver and vehicle.

Do you work on Christmas Day, bank holidays and weekends?+

Yes. 365 days a year includes Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Day and all bank holidays. The price does not change on bank holidays — our fixed £2.50/km rate applies every day of the year. We recommend booking Christmas and New Year transfers well in advance as slots fill quickly.

How far in advance should I book?+

For regular transfers, 24–48 hours notice is usually sufficient. For peak periods — school summer holidays (July–August), Christmas and New Year, Easter and May bank holidays — we recommend 1–3 weeks advance booking. Early morning slots (3am–6am) to Gatwick and Heathrow during school holidays fill up particularly quickly. As a rule, book as early as you know your travel dates.

Service

Flight Tracking & Service Details

Do you track flights for delays?+

Yes, always. We monitor your flight number from the moment it departs its origin airport. If your inbound flight from Barcelona is delayed by 2 hours, we know before you do and your driver adjusts automatically. You are never charged extra for delays caused by your airline. We ask for your flight number at the time of booking and it is monitored actively until you are collected.

Where does the driver meet me when I arrive back at the airport?+

Your driver meets you inside the terminal arrivals hall with a name board bearing your name. Not in a car park, not at a roadside pick-up zone — in the arrivals hall itself. You walk out of customs and your driver is waiting. For Gatwick, we specify North or South Terminal. For Heathrow, we go to the arrivals area of your specific terminal (T2, T3, T4 or T5). For Luton, we meet you inside and then collect the vehicle from the MSCP.

Do you provide child seats?+

Yes, free of charge. We carry infant carriers (rearward-facing for newborns to approximately 13kg), forward-facing child seats (approximately 9kg–18kg) and high-back booster seats (approximately 15kg–36kg). Tell us the ages and approximate weights of the children when booking and we will fit the correct seats before the driver arrives at your address.

What areas do you collect from?+

We collect from Langton Green, Tunbridge Wells, Tonbridge, Sevenoaks, Edenbridge, Penshurst, Speldhurst, Groombridge, Rusthall, Bidborough, Southborough, Crowborough, Uckfield, Forest Row, East Grinstead and surrounding villages in Kent, Surrey and East Sussex. If you are unsure whether we cover your address, call or WhatsApp us — we confirm within minutes.

How much luggage can I bring?+

Tell us your passenger count and luggage load when booking. A saloon comfortably handles 2–3 large suitcases plus hand luggage. An estate handles 4–5 large suitcases. An MPV is ideal for groups with 6+ large cases. We recommend the correct vehicle based on your luggage — there is no additional luggage charge, but you do need the right size vehicle for everything to fit safely and comfortably.

What if my plans change after I have booked?+

Contact us as soon as possible. Flight number changes are updated in our tracking immediately. Date or time changes are accommodated where availability allows. For cancellations with 24 hours or more notice, any deposit taken is refunded in full. For cancellations with less than 24 hours notice on peak-period bookings, we may retain the deposit to cover the driver commitment.

Airports & Ports

Airports & Cruise Ports

Which airports do you cover?+

We cover all five London airports: Gatwick (North and South Terminal), Heathrow (Terminals 2, 3, 4 and 5), Stansted, Luton and London City Airport. We also serve Dover Cruise Port (Eastern Docks) and Southampton Cruise Port (all four terminals).

Do you do cruise port transfers?+

Yes. We regularly transfer passengers to Dover Cruise Port (Eastern Docks — P&O, MSC, DFDS, Fred. Olsen) and Southampton Cruise Port (all four terminals — Cunard, Royal Caribbean, Celebrity, P&O, Norwegian and others). We track ship departure and arrival times the same way we track flights, and we handle large luggage volumes as standard.

Can you take me to Eurostar at Ashford or St Pancras?+

Yes. Ashford International is a straightforward run south on the A21 and A20 — approximately 35 miles from Langton Green. St Pancras International in London is accessible via the A21 and A20 into south-east London. Call us with your Eurostar departure details and we will confirm the price and pickup time.
