London City — The Business Traveller's Airport from Kent

London City Airport occupies a unique position among the five London airports. Compact, efficient and located in the Royal Docks area of east London, it operates primarily short-haul business routes to Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Dublin, Brussels, Edinburgh and Zurich. It is the preferred airport for professionals flying from the Tunbridge Wells and Langton Green area who prioritise speed of processing over the destination range of Heathrow or the price advantage of Gatwick.

Check-in at London City opens just 2 hours before departure and the airport itself is navigable in under 20 minutes from kerb to gate — a stark contrast to Heathrow's sprawling terminal system. The tradeoff is the route into east London, which requires navigating through or around the city rather than along clear motorway corridors.

The Route to London City — A21 and the Blackwall Tunnel

The most direct route from Langton Green to London City Airport avoids the M25 entirely. We head north on the A21 through Sevenoaks, join the A20 at Swanley and head into south-east London, using the Blackwall Tunnel to cross under the Thames and emerging into the Docklands area just minutes from the airport on the A13.

The Blackwall Tunnel is the critical variable on this route. It operates one-way alternating flow for northbound and southbound vehicles, and closures for maintenance — which happen overnight and sometimes during early morning hours — can force a diversion via the Rotherhithe Tunnel or further east via the Dartford Crossing. We check tunnel status before every London City transfer.

For early morning business flights (the 6am–8am departures to Amsterdam Schiphol and Frankfurt are popular with City workers based in Kent), the Blackwall Tunnel is typically clear and the journey from Langton Green takes 60–65 minutes. This makes a 4:30am or 5am pickup reasonable for a 7am departure.

Airlines and Routes from London City Airport

London City's route network is deliberately focused on business-friendly short-haul destinations with high frequency. Key routes that travellers from Tunbridge Wells regularly use include:

  • Amsterdam Schiphol (KLM, British Airways): Multiple daily departures. Strong connecting hub for onward long-haul travel.
  • Dublin (Aer Lingus, British Airways): Business and leisure. Dublin Airport also acts as a US pre-clearance hub.
  • Frankfurt (Lufthansa, British Airways): Financial sector route. Multiple daily departures.
  • Edinburgh and Glasgow (British Airways): UK domestic business travel from the most convenient London airport.
  • Zurich (SWISS): Direct service, popular with financial and legal sector travellers.
  • New York JFK (British Airways, select dates): The airport's flagship long-haul route using the Airbus A318 single-aisle configuration.
